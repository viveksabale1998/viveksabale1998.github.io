Science thrives on the exchange of ideas, multidisciplinary perspectives, and collaborative exploration. I actively collaborate with theoretical physicists, computational scientists, and experimental quantum groups across the globe.

Whether you are a researcher, faculty member, postdoctoral fellow, or student interested in joint projects, theoretical modeling, numerical simulations, or discussions, I welcome the opportunity to connect!

---

## Areas of Collaborative Interest

I am particularly excited to collaborate on topics at the intersection of:

### 1. Open Quantum Systems & Non-Markovian Reservoir Engineering
- Diagnosing memory effects, information backflow, and CP-divisibility breakdown in physical quantum channels.
- Correlated noise in multi-qubit registers and shared structured baths.
- Microscopic system-bath modeling (collective dissipation, subradiance, entanglement sudden death and revivals).

### 2. Quantum Machine Learning (QML) & Quantum State Diagnosis
- Quantum Support Vector Machines (QSVM) and quantum kernel methods for cross-domain state classification.
- Variational Quantum Neural Networks (QNN) and parameterized quantum circuits for entanglement verification.
- Mitigating barren plateaus and scaling QML algorithms for NISQ devices.

### 3. Quantum Batteries & Quantum Thermodynamics
- Work extraction, ergotropy maximization, and charging dynamics in Heisenberg spin chains and networks.
- Exploiting anisotropic interactions, such as the Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya interaction (DMI), to unlock super-extensive charging advantages.
- Open-system quantum thermodynamics and autonomous thermal machines.

### 4. Quantum Error Mitigation & Quantum Communication Protocols
- Active noise suppression protocols using weak measurements and quantum measurement reversal (QMR).
- Enhancing teleportation fidelity and fidelity deviation under dissipative environments.
- Quantum repeaters and distributed quantum information processing.

## How We Can Work Together

* **Joint Research Publications**: Formulating original theoretical models, deriving analytical solutions, and preparing high-impact manuscripts.
* **Numerical Simulations & Open-Source Code**: High-performance quantum simulation, numerical diagonalization, and quantum circuit implementations (Qiskit, Pennylane, Python).
* **Workshops & Reading Groups**: Sharing insights through seminars, workshops, and quantum computing training sessions.
* **Student Mentoring & Peer Discussions**: Brainstorming with graduate and undergraduate students interested in quantum information science.
