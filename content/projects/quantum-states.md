# Quantum States Data Set

This open dataset provides multi-qubit density matrices categorized by their separability and entanglement characteristics, specially formatted for benchmarking quantum machine learning models, state classifiers, and entanglement witnesses.

## 📊 Dataset Contents

- **Two-Qubit States**: Werner states, Horodecki states, Bell diagonal states, and Maximally Entangled Mixed States (MEMS).
- **Three-Qubit States**: Genuine tripartite entangled states (GHZ, W), biseparable states, and fully separable states.
- **Parameters Provided**: Density matrices ($\rho$), concurrence, negativity, purity, and entanglement spectrum eigenvalues.
