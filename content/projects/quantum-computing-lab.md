# Quantum Computing Lab Notebooks

Comprehensive, open-access laboratory notebooks designed for students and researchers exploring fundamental and intermediate quantum computation, circuit composition, quantum algorithms, and introductory quantum machine learning.

## 📌 Covered Topics

- **Quantum Fundamentals**: Superposition, entanglement, Bell state verification, and quantum teleportation.
- **Quantum Algorithms**: Deutsch-Jozsa, Grover search algorithm, and Quantum Phase Estimation (QPE).
- **Quantum Machine Learning**: Quantum feature encoding, parameterised circuits, and variational state classification.
