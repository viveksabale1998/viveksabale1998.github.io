# Quantum Battery Hamiltonian Simulations

Open-source numerical simulations of many-body quantum battery systems investigating charging dynamics, extractable work (ergotropy), and capacity speedup enabled by Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya interaction (DMI) and Heisenberg XXZ couplings.

## 📌 Key Highlights

- **Spin Chain Networks**: Simulates finite-size spin-1/2 Heisenberg spin chains driven by external charging fields.
- **DMI Effects**: Demonstrates how asymmetric exchange interactions boost charging power and suppress energy backflow.
- **Ergotropy Analysis**: Quantifies maximal work extraction using unitaries applied to the charged battery state.
